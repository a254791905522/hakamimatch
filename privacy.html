Effective date: March 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Hakimi Match is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is a general audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear app data.

6. Your Choices

We use reasonable measures to protect app data and maintain app integrity.
